# Process results and engineering interpretation

Source: Fall 2025 Micromachining final report, sections 2–3 and Tables 1–2. Measurements are reported academic lab results, including labmate contributions; confirm permission before public redistribution. This analysis was prepared retrospectively for the portfolio.

| Metric | Observation | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Pt linewidth | 197.6–197.7 µm vs 200 µm nominal | 1.15–1.20% below nominal at two inspected features; not a wafer-wide capability estimate. |
| PECVD oxide | 233.9–238.8 nm at five locations | 4.9 nm span; descriptive spatial variation only. |
| Laser holes | 856.9–908.2 µm across four axes | 9.18–14.31% below the 1 mm nominal; investigate dimensional bias. |
| EIS | Linear conductance–conductivity response reported | Functional validation under tested conditions, not repeatability or yield evidence. |

## Proposed follow-up

- Check mask dimensions and measurement calibration before attributing linewidth deviations to lithography.
- Compare oxide thickness measurements across additional wafers before making process stability claims.
- Investigate laser-hole dimensional bias with supervised process trials and dimensional inspection.
- Obtain original EIS data and repeat measurements before quantifying calibration uncertainty.

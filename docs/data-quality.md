# Data quality and limitations

Source: Fall 2025 final micromachining report, Tables 1–2 and Sections 2–3.

- Table 2 lists five oxide thickness measurements. The printed mean (235.3 nm) and standard deviation (1.9 nm) do not match the recomputed mean (236.34 nm) and sample standard deviation (2.11 nm). Verify the original instrument data before reporting summary statistics.
- The abstract describes quartz, while the methods identify borosilicate glass. Confirm substrate identity before making a specific material claim.
- The metal thickness reading of 117.3 µm in the methods is inconsistent with the nominal 60 nm stack. Verify units and measurement before citing it.
- Figure 7 shows a linear calibration with three points; raw impedance data are not supplied. Do not infer repeatability, yield, or independent measurement accuracy from this plot.
- The report acknowledges labmate-supplied figures and data. Confirm individual contributions and obtain permission before sharing original images or reports publicly.
- Table 1 reports five photoresist thickness readings (11,747–11,907 Å). Their recomputed mean is 11,856.2 Å. The original table reports a mean of 11,856 Å and standard deviation of 68.3 Å; retain raw readings for verification.
- The 224.3 µm developed-resist feature and 197.6–197.7 µm post-lift-off metal features are distinct process-stage observations; do not infer a paired process bias without confirming measurement locations.

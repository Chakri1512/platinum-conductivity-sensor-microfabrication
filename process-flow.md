# Fabrication and metrology workflow

University of Utah Micromachining Laboratory, Fall 2025. The following summarizes the course workflow and final project report; it does not establish independent operation of every tool.

| Lab | Process | Equipment / method | Project evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Photomask preparation | Heidelberg MicroPG 101; two masks | Electrode/interconnect and oxide-opening layouts |
| 2 | First photolithography | S1813, AZ 1:1 developer, SUSS contact aligner, Nanospec 3000 | Five resist thickness readings; representative developed feature 224.3 µm |
| 3 | Metal deposition | Denton 635 sputtering, Tencor P-20H profilometer | Target stack 10 nm Cr / 50 nm Pt; actual thickness reading requires unit verification |
| 4 | Lift-off | Resist removal and optical microscopy | Final Pt linewidth 197.6–197.7 µm versus 200 µm design |
| 5 | Dielectric deposition | Oxford PlasmaPro PECVD, optical thickness metrology | Five oxide readings, 233.9–238.8 nm |
| 6 | Second photolithography | Mask alignment and optical inspection | Window widths 197.9 and 202 µm |
| 7 | Oxide wet etch | Selective oxide removal and profilometry | Exposed electrodes with insulated wiring; reported step height 2053.5 Å |
| 8 | Laser machining | CO₂ laser, optical microscopy | Fluid port dimensions documented in README |
| 9 | Device assembly | Disco DAD641 dicing and soldering | Separated devices and electrical connections |
| 10 | Functional characterization | Gamry Interface 1000E, four-electrode EIS | Linear calibration reported over three plotted points |

Sources: final report, methods and results; weekly Lab 1–10 course materials. Course handouts are not redistributed here. Target thicknesses are not measured thicknesses. See [data quality notes](data-quality.md).
